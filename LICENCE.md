## 🧩 About

**World of Warcraft Helper** is a comprehensive overlay tool for World of Warcraft featuring cooldown tracker, loot radar, HUD enhancements, and map markers.

Based on popular addons like **WeakAuras**, **DBM**, and **Questie**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core HUD
- Cooldown Tracker — monitor spell and ability cooldowns
- Loot Radar — highlight nearby lootable corpses and chests
- Resource Bars — custom health, mana, and energy overlays
- Map Markers — pinpoint quest objectives and rare spawns

### 🗺️ Utility
- HUD Overlay — customizable on-screen info panels
- Inventory Helper — quick access to bag and bank items
- Chat Enhancer — timestamps and channel filters

### 🏋️ Training & Info
- DPS Meter — track damage and healing output
- Threat Meter — monitor threat levels in groups
- Raid Warnings — visual and audio alerts for boss mechanics
